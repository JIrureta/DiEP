Φ at step 0 should be `sqrt(1e-8) = 1e-4`: the search forward at `alpha = 0, beta = 1` is the original model exactly. With 32 calibration sequences an epoch is 2 steps, so the paper's 10 epochs are 20 steps here (80 in the paper): alpha and beta move very little from their initialisation, and the ranking is decided by small differences.

## 7. Reading this fairly

- **Same data, same protocol.** Every method here used the same 32 × 1024 C4 tokens and the same lm-eval configuration (the test suite checks DiEP's protocol constants against the GA's file).
- **Not the same compute.** DiEP's search is 20 steps of α/β (minutes); the GA evaluates ~1,600 candidates; DPMA trains ~20k structural parameters for 900 steps and repairs dropped experts (absorb/merge), which DiEP does not (pure pruning).
- **Seeds.** DiEP and DPMA v3 at keep 0.5 have three seeds each; everything else is one run, and random has five draws. A difference smaller than the random spread is not a result.
- **Compute per token.** DiEP and `dpma-v3-hard` run at most 8 experts per token; `dpma-v3` (soft export) runs more (9.2 at keep 0.5), so it is not compute-matched.
